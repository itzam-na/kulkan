# Local Audio Library

This context covers a listener's local audio collection and the releases and tracks they select for playback.

## Language

**Album**:
A music release that groups one or more tracks; a single is a one-track album.

**Album artist credit**:
The artist or artists attributed to a release, distinct from the artist credits attributed to its individual tracks.

**Artist credit**:
The artist or artists attributed to a track.

**Criterion**:
A condition the listener uses to identify tracks in the library.

**Library**:
The listener's local catalog of Library entries, discovered from one or more Library folders.

**Library workspace**:
The central area of Kulkán for viewing and searching the Library.

**Library entry**:
A file-backed item in the local catalog, one per resolved file path. Identical audio at distinct paths is represented by separate entries.

**Library folder**:
A filesystem folder selected as a source for explicitly scanning entries into the Library.
_Avoid_: Library root

**List playback behavior**:
The preference defining what happens when a track in a Library List is double-clicked.

**No Album**:
A placeholder grouping for tracks with no album release identity; it is not itself an album.

**Not-yet-played**:
A track with no qualifying plays in the player's local play history.

**Play count**:
The number of qualifying plays recorded for a library entry by the player. An unknown count is treated as zero.

**Playback attempt**:
A playback of one Track that may be paused and resumed. It ends when playback is stopped, another Track is selected, the app closes, or repeat starts a new pass.

**Qualifying play**:
A Playback attempt in which the listener hears the configured share of distinct Track content. An attempt can qualify at most once.

**Queue**:
An ordered sequence of Queue entries selected for playback.

**Queue entry**:
One occurrence of a Library entry in a Queue; the same Library entry may appear more than once.

**Single**:
A music release consisting of one track.

**Track**:
A distinct audio item in the listener's collection. A move or rename can preserve its identity when the audio content uniquely matches an existing entry.
